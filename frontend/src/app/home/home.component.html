GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Some quick answers to help you get started.

Is DiseaseSense a medical diagnosis?
No. DiseaseSense is designed to provide disease-risk insights and general awareness. It does not replace a doctor's diagnosis or professional medical advice.
Why might location be useful?
Location can help show relevant regional health information. Share it only when needed, and review the app's privacy information to understand how your data is handled.
When will I receive alerts?
Alerts depend on the monitoring and notification features available in the application. This preview does not mean that email or SMS notifications are already enabled.
